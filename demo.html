The Origin

From Makrana, Rajasthan
to 2 Million+ strong.

A small-town boy who prepared for medical school — then followed his passion for automobiles. In 2011, when no one was making Hindi automotive content on YouTube, Gagan saw a gap that millions of Indians needed filled.

"English was a huge hurdle that many faced in India when it came to understanding automobiles." He chose to speak the language of the buyer — not the boardroom.

Today, GaadiFy is India's second most-trusted automobile publication and usually the most-viewed one. With 1,400+ honest reviews, a team of 4, and a philosophy that a vehicle's soul lies in its powertrain and chassis — not cosmetics.

Safety First. Hamesha.

Every review starts with NCAP ratings and structural integrity — because no feature matters more than getting home safe.

Full Transparency

Every video discloses how the vehicle was sourced, testing methodology, and whether content is paid. No exceptions, for 2+ years running.

Street-Level Research

Visits local garages, talks to mechanics and owners directly. "The problems of Indian consumers are much different from what we see on the internet."

Hindi-First, Always

Speaking the buyer's language — not the boardroom's. Pioneered Hindi automotive content when no one else would.
